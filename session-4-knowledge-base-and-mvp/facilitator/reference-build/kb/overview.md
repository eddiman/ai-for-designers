# Bus-app project overview

## Goal

Help people catch the bus by making real-time information trustworthy enough to support an informed travel decision.

## Committed problem

> How might we make real-time bus information trustworthy enough that riders feel confident deciding whether to wait, leave, or choose another option?

The problem framing is recorded in [decisions/committed-problem.md](decisions/committed-problem.md).

## Current design context

- The research identifies broken trust in arrival information and uncertainty as the primary problem. See [learnings/research-insights.md](learnings/research-insights.md).
- The project uses three evidence-grounded **persona hypotheses**: the Verification-Seeking Planner, Simplicity-First Waiter, and Context-Dependent Rider. See [design/personas.md](design/personas.md) and [stories/persona-needs.md](stories/persona-needs.md).
- Three unselected prototype directions explore the problem: Signal, Decide, and Shift. Their concepts and flows are in [design/prototype-directions-and-flows.md](design/prototype-directions-and-flows.md).
- The persona UX review records strengths, gaps, and recommended changes across those directions. See [learnings/persona-ux-review.md](learnings/persona-ux-review.md).

## Current state

The supplied artifacts define a committed problem and three prototype directions, but do not record a chosen direction. The UX-review artifact includes row-level recommendations; its final triage section is blank. A direction still needs to be selected before the MVP spec is written.
