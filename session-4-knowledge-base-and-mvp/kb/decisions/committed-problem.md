# Committed problem

## Status

Committed in the supplied problem-definition and prototype artifacts.

## Decision

> How might we make real-time bus information trustworthy enough that riders feel confident deciding whether to wait, leave, or choose another option?

## Rationale

- All three interview summaries describe unreliable or misleading real-time information as a main frustration.
- In the questionnaire, 31% reported not knowing whether the bus was coming, 26% reported schedule mismatch, and 17% reported incorrect real-time information. The source groups these as about 74% of frustration tied to timing reliability.
- The questionnaire reports that 55% of respondents do not trust current real-time information, while 79% say they want to reliably know the arrival time.
- The research also shows that riders need to make a decision when information is uncertain, not only receive a countdown.

## Consequences

- The first problem to solve is confidence in current bus information and its use in a decision.
- The framing does not prescribe an app, notification system, display redesign, or personalized assistant.
- The prototype directions explore different solution approaches, but none is recorded as selected. See [../design/prototype-directions-and-flows.md](../design/prototype-directions-and-flows.md).
