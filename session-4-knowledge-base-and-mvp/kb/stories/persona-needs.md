# Persona-derived user outcomes

These are concise outcome statements derived from the persona hypotheses. They are not independently validated requirements.

## Verification-Seeking Planner

**As a rider who cross-checks travel information, I need** decision-ready information with visible confidence and uncertainty **so that** I can decide whether to wait, leave, or take an alternative without reconstructing the situation myself.

## Simplicity-First Waiter

**As a rider who wants a quick answer, I need** a simple, trustworthy indication of whether the bus is coming and when **so that** I can act without managing a feature-heavy app.

## Context-Dependent Rider

**As a rider whose needs change with the trip, I need** minimal information for familiar travel and more guidance when uncertainty rises **so that** I receive support without constant interruption.
